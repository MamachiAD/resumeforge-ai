    Questions, answered

    How is the price worked out?

    Price = time rate x quantity x reach x placement, plus small add-ons. Every step up in reach or placement costs more, and longer durations cost less per hour.

    Is there a spending limit?

    No. You can buy as many hours, days, weeks, months or years as you like. PayPal may apply its own limits to a single payment.

    When does my campaign start, and when are posts made?

    It starts when we approve it, normally soon after you pay. You see posts per day and posting times (UTC) before you pay, and you get an email after each post.

    Will the text be the same on every platform?

    You choose. Different text on each platform is the default; you can switch to identical text.

    Can I post on my own social accounts too?

    Yes, at checkout. Today we support Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram and Discord. We post only your own ad, with your own link, only for the days you paid for, and delete your connection when it ends.

    What if my ad is not approved?

    You are refunded in full automatically.

    Do you guarantee results?

    No. We guarantee your ad is posted as scheduled, and we show posts and link clicks so you can judge for yourself.
